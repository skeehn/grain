# Governance

Grain is currently maintainer-led. Maintainers merge changes, cut releases, and
make security decisions. Material runtime, storage, permission, protocol, and
compatibility decisions require an architecture decision record and review from
another maintainer when one is available.

Contributor status is earned through sustained, reviewed work; it is never tied
to payment or benchmark results. Release gates cannot be waived silently. A gate
exception must be documented in the release notes with its risk and rollback.

Before 1.0 the project will publish named maintainers, a conflict-resolution
process, and key-person continuity procedures.
