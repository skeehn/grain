# Code of Conduct

Grain contributors must keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and safe.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable. Discuss technical decisions on their evidence and
impact, assume good faith where reasonable, and stop an interaction when asked.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact or
GitHub moderation tools. Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban repeated or severe abuse.
Enforcement decisions should protect reporters' privacy and document conflicts of
interest.
